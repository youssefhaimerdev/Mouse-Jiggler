Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2025. This policy explains what data StayAwake collects (very little) and why.

Overview

StayAwake ("we", "us", or "our") operates the website located at https://stayawake.app. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our free online mouse jiggler tool.

Short version: The mouse jiggler runs entirely in your browser. We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal data. We use Google Analytics 4 (anonymized) and Google AdSense, both of which have their own privacy policies.

Information We Do NOT Collect

StayAwake does not collect or store:

  • Your name, email address, or any personally identifiable information
  • Your IP address (GA4 is configured with IP anonymization enabled)
  • Any information about your mouse position, movement data, or screen content
  • Login credentials of any kind (we have no accounts system)
  • Any data from your employer, device, or corporate network

Information Collected Automatically

Google Analytics 4

We use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to understand how visitors use our website in aggregate. GA4 collects:

  • Pages visited and time spent on each page
  • Approximate geographic location (country/region level only — we enable IP anonymization)
  • Browser type, operating system, and device category
  • Referring website and search terms (where available)
  • Custom events: jiggler_started, jiggler_stopped, interval_changed, page_engaged — these contain no personal data

This data is processed by Google under their Privacy Policy. You can opt out of GA tracking by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Google AdSense

We display advertising through Google AdSense. Google AdSense uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings. You can also opt out through the Network Advertising Initiative opt-out page.

For more information about how Google uses data from sites that use its services, visit: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Cookies

StayAwake itself uses one cookie:

  • sa-theme — stores your light/dark mode preference. This is a strictly functional cookie. It contains no personal data and is stored only on your device.

Google Analytics and Google AdSense set their own cookies. These are governed by Google's privacy policy. You may disable cookies in your browser settings, though this may affect the display of advertisements.

Local Storage

StayAwake stores your theme preference (sa-theme) in your browser's localStorage. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

Third-Party Links

Our site may contain links to external websites (e.g., hardware jiggler product pages, Google's privacy tools). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites.

Children's Privacy

StayAwake is not directed at children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child, please contact us at hello@stayawake.app.

Your Rights (GDPR — EU/EEA Users)

If you are located in the European Union or European Economic Area, you have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), including:

  • The right to access information we hold about you (we hold none)
  • The right to data portability
  • The right to erasure ("right to be forgotten")
  • The right to object to processing
  • The right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

Because we collect no personal data from users directly, most of these rights are fulfilled by default. For Google Analytics and AdSense data, please refer to Google's GDPR compliance resources.

Your Rights (CCPA — California Users)

If you are a California resident, you have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). Since StayAwake does not sell personal information and does not collect personal information in the traditional sense, most CCPA provisions are satisfied by default. For questions, contact hello@stayawake.app.

Security

StayAwake is served exclusively over HTTPS. This encrypts all data in transit between your browser and our servers. The mouse jiggler tool itself runs client-side and establishes no server connections during use.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: hello@stayawake.app
