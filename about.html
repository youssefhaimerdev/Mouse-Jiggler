About

About StayAwake

A free tool built for the millions of people who work from home and just want their computer to stay awake.

StayAwake was built to solve a simple but frustrating problem: your computer goes to sleep, your Slack shows Away, and suddenly you're explaining yourself. You shouldn't need a $40 USB gadget or a sketchy software download to prevent that.

Our Mission

We believe utility tools should be free, fast, and simple. StayAwake is a single-purpose tool that does one thing and does it well — keeps your computer awake while you're working. No upsells, no premium plans, no email capture. Open the page, click Start, done.

The site is maintained as a free public utility and supported by non-intrusive display advertising. Every dollar from ads goes directly into keeping the servers running and improving the tool.

What We Built

StayAwake uses a combination of three browser-native techniques to prevent idle detection:

  • Wake Lock API — directly requests the OS keep the screen active (Chrome & Edge)
  • Canvas animation loop — keeps the browser rendering thread active
  • Pointer event simulation — dispatches synthetic mouse events at your chosen interval

The tool is open about exactly which technique is running at any given moment. Read the full technical explanation on our How It Works page.

Privacy Commitment

We have a simple privacy policy: we don't know who you are and we don't want to. StayAwake collects no personally identifiable information.
