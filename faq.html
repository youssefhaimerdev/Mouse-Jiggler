Mouse Jiggler FAQ

15 of the most common questions about StayAwake and browser-based mouse jigglers — answered clearly.

The Basics
A mouse jiggler is a tool that simulates continuous mouse movement to prevent your computer from entering an idle state. When your computer detects no input for a set period, it locks the screen, activates a screensaver, or shows you as "Away" on Slack and Teams. A mouse jiggler prevents this by generating activity the OS recognizes as real input — keeping your screen on and status active. StayAwake does all of this in your browser, with no download needed.

Privacy & Safety
StayAwake installs nothing on your device and appears only as a browser tab. It will not appear in Windows Task Manager, macOS Activity Monitor, or standard endpoint monitoring lists. However, some advanced enterprise monitoring tools (Teramind, ActivTrak, Veriato) analyze typing patterns, mouse movement velocity, or active application focus — these tools operate differently from simple idle detection and may detect anomalies. Use StayAwake responsibly and in accordance with your employer's policies.
Completely safe. StayAwake collects zero personal data. The jiggler runs entirely in your browser with no server communication. We use Google Analytics 4 with IP anonymization for aggregate traffic statistics (page views, country-level data), and Google AdSense for display advertising. Neither service receives personally identifiable information from us. See the full Privacy Policy.
The tool itself is legal everywhere. Whether using it is ethical depends on your situation. Using it to maintain accurate availability status while you're genuinely working (reading, on a call, thinking) is reasonable — the OS idle timer often misrepresents real work. Using it to appear active while not working could violate your employment agreement. We're not here to judge, but we do encourage responsible use.
Technical Questions

The jiggling techniques themselves use negligible CPU (less than 0.1% on modern hardware). On battery, expect roughly the same drain as having a browser tab open with a video paused.
